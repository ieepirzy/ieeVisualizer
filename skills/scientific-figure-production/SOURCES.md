# Sources: scientific-figure-production

Format of every entry: title and URL, `Licence:`, `Use:` (`adapted` = text
paraphrased and restructured under the licence's terms; `cited` = idea or
finding only, no text taken).

- **K-Dense, `scientific-visualization` skill** <https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/tree/main/skills/scientific-visualization> (commit a1043ef)
  - Licence: MIT
  - Use: adapted — define medium and final width first; record transformations and provenance with the export; inspect delivered files rather than trusting defaults; verify publisher rules live; grayscale screening is not a full accessibility test. Paraphrased.
- **N. P. Rougier, M. Droettboom, P. E. Bourne, "Ten Simple Rules for Better Figures", PLOS Comput Biol 10(9), 2014** <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003833>
  - Licence: CC0-1.0
  - Use: adapted — know the audience and medium, captions are not optional, do not trust the defaults, avoid chartjunk.
- **T. L. Weissgerber, N. M. Milic, S. J. Winham, V. D. Garovic, "Beyond Bar and Line Graphs: Time for a New Data Presentation Paradigm", PLOS Biol 13(4), 2015** <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002128>
  - Licence: CC-BY-4.0
  - Use: adapted — bar graphs of means hide distributions; show individual points for small samples. Paraphrased; the 15–20 point figure is our guidance, not theirs.
