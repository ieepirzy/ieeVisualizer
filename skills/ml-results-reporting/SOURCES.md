# Sources: ml-results-reporting

Format of every entry: title and URL, `Licence:`, `Use:` (`adapted` = text
paraphrased and restructured under the licence's terms; `cited` = idea or
finding only, no text taken).

- **R. Agarwal, M. Schwarzer, P. S. Castro, A. Courville, M. G. Bellemare, "Deep Reinforcement Learning at the Edge of the Statistical Precipice", NeurIPS 2021** <https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264>
  - Licence: CC-BY-4.0
  - Use: adapted — IQM, stratified bootstrap confidence intervals, performance profiles, unreliability of point estimates from few runs. Paraphrased.
- **google-research/rliable** <https://github.com/google-research/rliable> (commit 3ccd9f4)
  - Licence: Apache-2.0
  - Use: cited — named as the library implementing the aggregates. No text or code copied.
- **J. Dodge, S. Gururangan, D. Card, R. Schwartz, N. A. Smith, "Show Your Work: Improved Reporting of Experimental Results", EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019** <https://aclanthology.org/D19-1224/>
  - Licence: CC-BY-4.0
  - Use: adapted — report hyperparameter search budget; expected validation performance as a function of budget. Paraphrased.
- **R. Tang, J. Lee, J. Xin, X. Liu, Y. Yu, J. Lin, "Showing Your Work Doesn't Always Work", ACL 2020** <https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.246>
  - Licence: CC-BY-4.0
  - Use: adapted — the original expected-max estimator is biased; report the number of trials. Paraphrased.
- **E. Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals: A Statistical Approach to Language Model Evaluations" (2024)** <https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640>
  - Licence: Unverified (arXiv default licence)
  - Use: cited — CLT standard errors for evals, clustered standard errors, paired comparisons. Stated in our own words; arithmetic example ours.
- **P. Henderson et al., "Deep Reinforcement Learning that Matters", AAAI 2018** <https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06560>
  - Licence: Unverified (arXiv default licence)
  - Use: cited — sensitivity of results to seeds and implementation details.
- **NeurIPS Paper Checklist** <https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist>
  - Licence: Copyrighted (web page)
  - Use: cited — report error bars with their source of variability and method.
