## Worked example: monitoring issue

> **Operations in line with last year, week to 4 October**
>
> **What changed:** 47 confirmed bookings (same week 2025: 45); on-time
> arrival 94% (2025: 93%).
> **Why it matters:** autumn capacity planning can proceed on the current
> crew roster.
> **What is uncertain:** booking export for week 38 failed; September totals
> exclude it.
> **What warrants action:** nothing this period.

No figures: two numbers and a comparison need none.

## Worked example: exception issue

> **Conversion fell to 24% as the lead mix shifted toward Meta ads**
> Every channel converted at least as well as in August.
>
> **What changed:** quote-to-booking conversion 24% in the week to 4 Oct, vs
> 31% the week before (412 quotes).
> **Why it matters:** roughly €12,000 of monthly revenue per lost point at
> current volume.
> **What is uncertain:** whether Meta leads convert later rather than not at
> all (median quote-to-booking lag is 9 days; the newest leads have not
> matured).
> **What warrants action:** decide whether to cap Meta spend until mature
> conversion is known (owner: Eemeli); recheck on 13 Oct.

The finding's evidence is one figure: conversion by channel as lines. The
channel mix goes in a table or a second figure only if the reader needs it.
